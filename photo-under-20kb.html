Recommended workflow

  1. Choose Custom Size.
  2. Enter the portal’s required width and height.
  3. Set Target KB to 20.
  4. Select JPG or WebP if accepted.
  5. Process and verify the result.

Quality trade-off

Very low file-size targets can reduce detail. Start with a clear source image and avoid repeatedly recompressing an already compressed file.

Verify official rules

The required pixel dimensions and format are just as important as the KB limit.
