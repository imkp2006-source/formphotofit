How to make a photo under 50 KB

  1. Select the Photo 50 KB preset.
  2. Upload your image.
  3. Choose custom dimensions when required.
  4. Click Resize & Compress and review the output size.

When the output is still above 50 KB

Try JPG or WebP, reduce the output dimensions, or use a simpler background. PNG photos often remain larger because PNG is optimized for graphics and transparency rather than ordinary photographs.

Do not rely on file size alone

Official forms may also require specific dimensions, format, background, and photo composition. Check every listed condition.
