FAQ

What is the photo size for SSC and UPSC forms?

A commonly requested starting point is 200 × 230 pixels, under 50 KB, in JPEG format on a plain background. Exact rules differ by notification year and exam body, so always confirm on the official form before submitting.

What signature size do SSC and UPSC forms usually ask for?

A small signature scan, often within a compact box and under 20 KB, is a typical requirement. Check the specific notification for exact pixel and size limits.

Can I prepare both photo and signature in one place?

Yes. FormPhotoFit lets you switch between a photo preset and a signature preset in the same free browser-based tool, so you can prepare both files for the same application without leaving the page.
